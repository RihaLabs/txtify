TXTify

Convert any file to TXT for AI chats

TXTify turns PDF, Word, Excel, code, data files, ZIP archives and whole folders into clean plain text that ChatGPT, Claude and other AI chats can read. Free and open source. It all runs in your browser, so your files never leave your computer.

Which files can TXTify convert?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, EPUB, Jupyter notebooks, code and text files, SAS, SPSS and Stata datasets, Parquet, SQLite, CSV, images with OCR, and archives such as ZIP, RAR and 7z. Whole folders work too.

Why convert files to TXT?

AI chats and agents often reject formats like XLSX or ZIP but always accept plain text, which also uses fewer tokens. Merge a whole project into one TXT and paste or upload it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

Is it safe for sensitive data?

Your files are processed inside your browser and are never uploaded. Only open-source converter libraries are downloaded from a CDN. For data files you can export just the structure, such as column names and types, without any values.

Is TXTify free?

Yes. It is free, open source (MIT) and needs no account or AI service. Supported data formats include SAS, Parquet, SQLite, Excel, CSV and Python pickle files.
